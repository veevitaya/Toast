# Cookie Policy

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you use a website or application. They are widely used to make services work more efficiently and to provide information to the operators of the service.

## 2. How Toast Uses Cookies and Similar Technologies

Toast uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and session storage) to:

### 2.1 Essential Functions

- **Authentication:** Keep you signed in and maintain your session
- **Preferences:** Remember your language, cuisine preferences, and display settings
- **Security:** Protect your account and prevent unauthorized access

### 2.2 Analytics and Performance

- **Usage Analytics:** Understand how you interact with the app to improve our service
- **Performance Monitoring:** Track app performance and identify technical issues
- **Feature Usage:** Measure which features are most valuable to users

### 2.3 Personalization

- **Taste Profile:** Store your food preference data to personalize recommendations
- **Location Preferences:** Remember your preferred search areas
- **Recent Activity:** Maintain your browsing history within the app for convenience

## 3. Types of Storage We Use

| Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|----------|
| Session Storage | Temporary session data | Until browser/app closes |
| Local Storage | Preferences and profile data | Until manually cleared |
| Cookies | Authentication tokens | Varies by purpose |

## 4. Third-Party Technologies

We may use third-party services that employ their own cookies and tracking technologies, including:

- LINE Platform (for authentication and social features)
- Analytics services (for usage analysis)

## 5. Managing Your Preferences

You can manage your cookie and storage preferences by:

- Clearing app data through your device settings
- Using the "Clear Data" option in Toast's profile settings
- Adjusting your device's privacy settings

Please note that disabling certain cookies or storage may affect the functionality of the Service.

## 6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the updated effective date at the top of this document.

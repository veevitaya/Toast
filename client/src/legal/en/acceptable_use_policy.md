# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") outlines the rules and guidelines for using the Toast platform. By using Toast, you agree to comply with this policy. Violations may result in account suspension or termination.

## 2. General Conduct

When using Toast, you agree to:

- Treat other users, restaurant staff, and partners with respect
- Provide honest and accurate information
- Use the platform for its intended purpose of food discovery and dining decisions
- Comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws

## 3. Prohibited Activities

You may not use Toast to:

### 3.1 Harmful Content

- Post content that is defamatory, obscene, threatening, or harassing
- Share content that promotes violence, discrimination, or hatred
- Upload content that infringes on intellectual property rights
- Distribute spam, malware, or other harmful material

### 3.2 Fraudulent Behavior

- Create fake accounts or impersonate others
- Post false or misleading restaurant reviews
- Manipulate ratings, reviews, or recommendation systems
- Engage in any form of fraud or deception

### 3.3 Platform Abuse

- Attempt to reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the app
- Use automated bots, scrapers, or crawlers
- Interfere with the proper functioning of the Service
- Circumvent security measures or access restrictions
- Exploit bugs or vulnerabilities

### 3.4 Commercial Misuse

- Use the platform for unauthorized commercial purposes
- Scrape or harvest user data
- Advertise or promote products or services without authorization
- Resell access to the Service

## 4. Restaurant Reviews and Content

When writing reviews or posting content:

- Be honest and base reviews on genuine experiences
- Do not accept compensation for reviews without disclosure
- Respect the privacy of other diners and restaurant staff
- Do not include personal contact information of others
- Keep feedback constructive and relevant

## 5. Group Sessions

During group dining sessions:

- Respect other participants' preferences and choices
- Do not use group features to spam or harass other users
- Only invite people who consent to participate

## 6. Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or behavior that violates this policy, please report it through the app or contact us at legal@toastapp.com.

## 7. Enforcement

We reserve the right to:

- Remove content that violates this policy
- Suspend or terminate accounts for violations
- Report illegal activity to appropriate authorities
- Take legal action for severe violations

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this AUP from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

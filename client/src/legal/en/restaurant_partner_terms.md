# Restaurant Partner Terms

## 1. Overview

These Restaurant Partner Terms ("Partner Terms") govern the relationship between Prodigy Dreamground Co., Ltd. ("ProDG," "Toast," "we," or "us") and restaurant owners, operators, or authorized representatives ("Partners," "you") who register and maintain a presence on the Toast platform.

## 2. Partner Registration

### 2.1 Eligibility

To register as a Partner, you must be a legally operating food establishment with valid licenses and permits as required by Thai law and local regulations.

### 2.2 Verification

We reserve the right to verify the legitimacy of your restaurant and may require documentation including business registration, food safety permits, and identification of authorized representatives.

## 3. Partner Responsibilities

As a Partner, you agree to:

- Provide accurate and up-to-date restaurant information including menu, pricing, hours, and location
- Maintain food safety and hygiene standards as required by applicable laws
- Respond to user reviews and feedback in a professional manner
- Not engage in fraudulent or misleading practices
- Keep your Partner dashboard information current

## 4. Listing and Content

### 4.1 Restaurant Listings

We will create and maintain a listing for your restaurant on the Toast platform based on the information you provide. You are responsible for the accuracy of this information.

### 4.2 Photos and Media

You grant Toast a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, display, and distribute photos and media you upload to represent your restaurant on the platform.

## 5. Promotions and Campaigns

Partners may create promotional campaigns through the Toast platform. All promotions must be honored as advertised and comply with applicable consumer protection laws.

## 6. Data and Analytics

We provide Partners with aggregated analytics about their restaurant's performance on the platform. We do not share individual user personal data with Partners.

## 7. Fees and Payments

Any applicable subscription fees, commission rates, or service charges will be communicated separately and agreed upon before activation. Fee schedules may be updated with reasonable notice.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. We may immediately suspend or terminate Partner access for violations of these terms or applicable laws.

## 9. Contact

For Partner-related inquiries, please contact legal@toastapp.com.

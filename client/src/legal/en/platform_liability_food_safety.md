# Platform Liability & Food Safety Disclaimer

## 1. Platform Role

Toast is a food discovery and decision-making platform that connects users with restaurant information. Toast does not operate, own, or control any restaurant listed on the platform. We are an information and technology service, not a food service provider.

## 2. Restaurant Information Accuracy

### 2.1 Information Sources

Restaurant information displayed on Toast is sourced from:

- Restaurant partners who self-report their information
- Publicly available data
- User-generated content and reviews
- Third-party data providers

### 2.2 Accuracy Disclaimer

While we strive to display accurate information, we cannot guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of:

- Menu items and pricing
- Operating hours and availability
- Allergen and dietary information
- Health and safety compliance status
- Photos and visual representations

Users should verify critical information directly with restaurants, especially regarding food allergies and dietary requirements.

## 3. Food Safety

### 3.1 Allergen Warning

**Important:** Toast does not guarantee the accuracy of allergen information displayed on the platform. If you have food allergies or intolerances, always confirm ingredients and preparation methods directly with the restaurant before ordering.

### 3.2 Hygiene and Standards

Toast is not responsible for the food safety, hygiene practices, or health code compliance of any restaurant listed on the platform. Restaurant partners are solely responsible for compliance with all applicable food safety laws and regulations.

## 4. Delivery Services

When Toast links to third-party delivery services (such as Grab, LINE MAN, or Robinhood), we are not responsible for:

- Order accuracy or fulfillment
- Food quality during delivery
- Delivery timing or reliability
- Pricing or fees charged by delivery platforms

## 5. Limitation of Liability

Toast, its officers, directors, employees, and agents shall not be liable for any health issues, allergic reactions, food poisoning, or other adverse effects resulting from dining at or ordering from restaurants discovered through the platform.

## 6. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- Verifying dietary and allergen information with restaurants
- Making informed dining decisions
- Reporting inaccurate information to us
- Exercising reasonable judgment when selecting restaurants

## 7. Reporting Issues

If you encounter food safety concerns or inaccurate restaurant information, please report it to us at legal@toastapp.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

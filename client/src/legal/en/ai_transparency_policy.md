# AI Recommendation Transparency Policy

## 1. Purpose

Toast is committed to transparency about how our AI-powered recommendation systems work. This policy explains the technology behind our personalized food discovery features and how decisions are made when suggesting restaurants and dishes to you.

## 2. How Our Recommendations Work

### 2.1 Taste DNA System

Toast builds a personalized "Taste DNA" profile based on your interactions with the app, including:

- **Swipe behavior:** Restaurants you like (swipe right) and pass on (swipe left)
- **Super likes:** Restaurants you show strong interest in (weighted more heavily)
- **Search patterns:** What you search for and explore
- **Time-of-day activity:** When you typically use the app
- **Quiz responses:** Your answers to our food preference quizzes

### 2.2 Recommendation Signals

Our recommendation engine considers multiple signals:

- Your cuisine preferences and dietary restrictions
- Your budget preferences
- Location proximity and accessibility
- Restaurant popularity and trending status
- Time-relevant factors (meal time, day of week)
- Group preferences during group sessions
- Seasonal and contextual relevance

### 2.3 Daily Craving Engine

Our "Daily Craving Engine" combines your taste profile with contextual data to generate timely, relevant suggestions. This system uses recency-weighted scoring, meaning recent preferences carry more influence than older ones.

## 3. What We Don't Do

- We do not use your data to discriminate based on protected characteristics
- We do not sell recommendation placement to restaurants (organic recommendations are not paid placements)
- We do not use sensitive personal data beyond food preferences for recommendations
- We do not make recommendations based on your financial status beyond your stated budget preference

## 4. Promotional Content

When restaurant partners run promotional campaigns, these may appear alongside organic recommendations. Promotional content is always clearly labeled and distinguished from organic recommendations.

## 5. Your Control

You can influence your recommendations by:

- Updating your cuisine preferences in your profile
- Adjusting dietary restrictions
- Using the "Refine my picks" feature
- Swiping to provide feedback on suggestions
- Resetting your taste profile through Settings

## 6. Continuous Improvement

We regularly evaluate and improve our recommendation algorithms to ensure fairness, accuracy, and relevance. We are committed to reducing bias and improving the quality of suggestions over time.

## 7. Contact

For questions about our AI systems, please contact privacy@toastapp.com.
